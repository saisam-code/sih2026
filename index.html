02 / HOW IT ACTUALLY WORKS

Seven moves. One strong solution.

01 — PROBLEM STATEMENTS

Start with the problem.

Understand the real need, users, constraints and context before jumping into technology.

02 — TEAM FORMATION

Build the right mix.

Development, AI/ML, UI/UX, research, domain knowledge and presentation skills can all matter.

03 — IDEATION

Turn insight into direction.

Compare approaches, challenge assumptions and choose a practical solution your team can execute.

04 — BUILDING

Make it real.

Build an MVP or prototype, test it with realistic scenarios and improve the weak parts quickly.

05 — SUBMISSION & SELECTION

Show the solution clearly.

Teams move through a submission and selection journey. Exact rules and criteria should be checked against official SIH information.

06 — HACKATHON

Execute under pressure.

Selected teams develop, test, refine and communicate their solution during the hackathon.

07 — PITCH & EVALUATION

Defend the idea.

Demonstrate the solution, explain impact and feasibility, discuss innovation and answer questions.
