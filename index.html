01 / SIH IN SIMPLE TERMS

What is Smart India Hackathon?

SIH is a problem-solving journey. Teams study a challenge, design a practical solution, build a prototype and communicate why it matters.

01 — PROBLEM

Start with a real challenge.

Understand the users, context, constraints and actual need before deciding what technology to use.

02 — TEAM

Bring different skills together.

A strong team can combine development, AI/ML, design, research, domain knowledge and communication.

03 — SOLUTION

Turn the idea into something testable.

Build a prototype or MVP, validate the important assumptions and improve the weak parts quickly.

04 / HOW TO BUILD A TEAM

Choose complementary skills.

Start with what the problem needs, not just the technologies you already know.

01 — TECH

Build the product.

Developers, AI/ML engineers, data specialists and IoT builders turn the concept into a working prototype.

02 — PRODUCT

Make it usable.

UI/UX, research and domain-focused members help the team understand users and create a practical experience.

03 — COMMUNICATION

Make the solution clear.

Strong explanation matters: problem, approach, technology, feasibility, impact and evidence should connect.

06 / PRACTICE

Think like a hackathon team.

Use this mini exercise before choosing technology.

SAMPLE SIH-STYLE PROBLEM
“How can technology improve waste management on college campuses?”

There is no single correct answer. Start by understanding who experiences the problem, why it happens and what evidence you would need.

Now test the idea against reality.

Ask: Who will use it? What changes for them? Can the team build a credible prototype? How will you measure whether it works?
